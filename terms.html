What Cheqo is — and isn't

  • Cheqo is an information tool that lists open class action settlements and helps you keep track of deadlines. It is not a law firm, is not affiliated with any court, settlement administrator or company named in the app, and does not provide legal advice.
  • Cheqo does not file claims for you. You file on the official settlement website, where filing is free.
  • Amounts shown are taken from court notices and are estimates only. Actual payments depend on the number of claims, the administrator's decision and court approval. Cheqo does not guarantee eligibility or any payment.
  • Only file claims you are honestly entitled to. Settlement forms are signed under penalty of perjury.
  • Deadlines and details can change; always confirm on the official site before filing.
