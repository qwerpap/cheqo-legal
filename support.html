Help and contact

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Reach us

Email netkaktakto@gmail.com. Useful to include: the settlement name, what looks wrong (deadline, amount, link), and your iOS version. We answer within two business days.

Quick answers

Why no settlements match me? The list is curated by hand from official administrator sites, and only verified entries appear. Try adding more brands or an earlier start year.

Is the amount guaranteed? No. It is taken from the court notice and the administrator decides.

Does Cheqo file for me? No. You get the official link and a field guide; you submit it yourself.

A reminder did not fire. Check that notifications are allowed for Cheqo in iOS Settings, and that the deadline is still ahead.

Restore a purchase. Open Settings in Cheqo and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID.

Cancel Pro. iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Cheqo, Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Spotted a settlement we missed?

Send the official administrator website. We add entries only after checking the deadline and eligibility there.
