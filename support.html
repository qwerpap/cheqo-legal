Cheqo: Class Action Payouts · updated October 3, 2026

Questions, a wrong deadline or amount, or a settlement we missed? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we reply within two business days.

Common questions

  • Does Cheqo file claims for me? No. Cheqo links to the official settlement site, where filing is free.
  • Will I get the amount shown? Not necessarily. Amounts come from court notices and are estimates; the administrator decides.
  • How do I cancel? Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Cheqo.
  • Restore a purchase: Cheqo › Settings › Restore purchases.
