Privacy Policy

Cheqo: Class Action Payouts · updated October 3, 2026

Cheqo is made by Danil Chshavelev. Short version: your answers stay on your iPhone, and Cheqo never sends them anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your questionnaire answers (the brands you picked, your state, the years and the data-breach answer) are stored only on your iPhone. Matching against the settlement list happens on the device.
  • Claims you mark as filed or paid, the amounts you type, and your reminders are stored only on your device.
  • Cheqo has no accounts and does not use location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The settlement list

Cheqo downloads a public list of open settlements from its own server (Cloudflare). The request carries no personal information — it is the same file for everyone. Cloudflare may log standard technical data (IP address, time) for security, per its own policy.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. To manage subscriptions and measure how the paywall performs, Cheqo uses Adapty as a processor. Adapty receives an anonymous app-generated profile ID, purchase and subscription status, device model, OS and app version, country/region derived from the IP address, which onboarding screen was reached, and three numbers: how many brands were picked, the year bucket and how many settlements matched. Your brands, state and filed claims are never sent. Cheqo does not collect your name, email or advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Notifications

Deadline reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Cheqo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting data

Remove a tracked claim from the Wallet, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. To ask about data held by Adapty for your anonymous profile, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com with the profile ID shown in the feedback email.
