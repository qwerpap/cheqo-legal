What Cheqo does with your data

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

There are no accounts. Your answers about brands, state and dates are used on the phone to match settlements and are never uploaded. The app talks to two outside services: our settlement-list server and Adapty for purchases.

1. What lives only on your iPhone

  • The questionnaire: which brands you picked, your state, since when you used them, and whether a data breach applies to you.
  • The claim wallet: settlements you marked as filed or paid and any amounts you typed in.
  • Reminder schedule. Deadline reminders are local notifications set by the app, three days and one day before a form closes. Notification permission is asked only when you turn reminders on, and no notification content is sent anywhere.

Cheqo does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data, and it has no sign-in.

2. The settlement list (our server)

When the app refreshes, it downloads one public file, claims.json, from a small Cloudflare Worker that we run. Every user gets the identical file and the request contains nothing from your questionnaire, no account and no device ID. As with any web request, Cloudflare sees the connection's IP address in its routine network logs. We do not store per-user records or use that address.

3. Purchases and Adapty

Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID. To read your subscription status and see how the paywall performs we use Adapty, which receives:

Sent to AdaptyWhy
A random profile ID generated by the appTies a purchase to this install, no name or email
Purchase and subscription status, trial stateUnlock Pro, restore purchases
Device model, OS and app version, country from IPPricing and diagnostics
The last onboarding screen reached; three counts: brands picked, year range, number of matchesPaywall measurement

The brand names, your state and your wallet entries are not part of that data. Adapty acts as our processor under its own privacy terms.

4. Official claim sites

Each settlement links to the administrator's own website, which opens outside Cheqo's control. If you file there, that site's privacy policy applies to whatever you type into its form. Cheqo does not see or relay it.

5. Sending feedback

If you use the feedback link in Settings, it opens your mail app with a draft that includes your anonymous profile ID and iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your mail app.

6. Deleting, children, changes

  • Delete a wallet entry in the app, or delete the app to erase everything stored on the phone.
  • To ask about the Adapty profile, email us the profile ID from the feedback draft.
  • Cheqo is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
  • If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
